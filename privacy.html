Privacy Policy

App: What's Inside?  |  Developer: Ihor Pikula  |  Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the game "What's Inside?" (the "App") handles. We keep it short because the App collects very little.

1. Information we do not collect

The App has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal details.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, upgrades, unlocked objects, settings, daily reward streak) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. If you delete the App, this data is removed.

3. Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use device information such as your advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads. On iOS, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in the system prompt ("Allow App to track your activity"). You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), we show a consent form before personalized ads are used.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove full-screen ads permanently with the in-app purchase "Remove Ads", or with the VIP subscription.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple tells the App only whether a purchase succeeded.

5. Analytics and diagnostics

The App may record anonymous gameplay events (for example, which level was completed) and crash diagnostics to improve the game. These events are not linked to your identity. If an analytics service is added in the future, this policy will be updated.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can reset all local data by deleting the App. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings, and manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
